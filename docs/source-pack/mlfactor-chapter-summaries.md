# Chapter Summaries

These are original, short summaries of the themes that matter most for the site.

## Why ML belongs in factor investing

- Financial data is noisy, small relative to model flexibility, and regime-dependent.
- Traditional linear models often miss interactions and nonlinear effects.
- The useful goal is usually better ranking, calibration, or risk awareness, not perfect prediction.
- A model can be helpful even when its point forecast is weak.
- The best output is often a distribution, confidence band, or scenario view.

## Why point estimates fail

- Market outcomes are distributions, not single paths.
- Analyst models often compress uncertainty into a false sense of precision.
- Small assumption changes can move valuation outputs materially.
- A single target price hides model sensitivity and path dependence.
- Investors should ask what would have to be true for the estimate to hold.

## Why validation matters

- Finance data is time-ordered, so validation must respect chronology.
- Random shuffles can create artificial skill.
- Leakage can enter through revisions, survivorship, or future information.
- Walk-forward validation is usually a better default than IID cross-validation.
- A simple stable model with honest validation can beat a flashy but brittle one.

## Why regimes matter

- Factor behaviour changes across inflation, growth, liquidity, and risk appetite regimes.
- A strategy that worked in one period may fail when market structure shifts.
- The goal is to learn when the model should be trusted and when it should be discounted.
- Similarity analysis is only useful when paired with differences and falsifiers.
- Regime framing should support judgment, not replace it.

## Why ensembles matter

- No single model deserves unconditional trust.
- Different models can specialize in different parts of the signal.
- Disagreement between models is itself useful information.
- Ensembling can reduce fragility if governance is strong.
- The combined output should be interpreted as a decision aid, not a prophecy.
